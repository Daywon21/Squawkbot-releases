Vega (SquawkBot) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026 · Product: Vega, the SquawkBot desktop app for Windows

Vega (SquawkBot) is desktop software that runs on your computer. It connects your streaming accounts so you can run chat, alerts, and OBS from one app. This policy describes what the app accesses and where that data stays.

Who controls the data

You run the app locally. Account tokens and settings are stored on your PC under your user profile in the .squawkbot folder. We do not operate a Vega or SquawkBot cloud account that receives your live chat.

Data we access (YouTube / Google)

When you connect YouTube, the app may access:

  • Your Google account email, name, and profile, to show which account is connected
  • Your YouTube channel id and channel name
  • Your live broadcasts and live chat, to show chat and run bot features while you are live
  • Permission to send live chat messages the bot or you post

The app requests openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile, youtube.readonly, and youtube.force-ssl. Live chat can also be read from YouTube’s public watch page (Innertube) for the broadcast you are on. The app does not read your private videos, playlists, or Google Drive.

Other connections

  • Twitch. Broadcaster and bot logins, chat, and EventSub, so chat and alerts work in the app and OBS.
  • Kick. Account login and chat for the channel you connect.
  • OBS. A local WebSocket connection on your PC (default 127.0.0.1:4455) to control scenes and browser sources. That traffic stays on your machine.

How data is used

  • Show and send live chat while you are streaming
  • Run alerts, goals, and overlays in OBS
  • Keep you signed in on this PC until you log out

We do not sell Google, YouTube, Twitch, or Kick data. We do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

  • Tokens and settings stay in the .squawkbot folder on your PC until you log out or delete that folder
  • Chat shown in the app is processed on your PC. It is not uploaded to a Vega or SquawkBot server

Your choices

  • Log out of YouTube, Twitch, or Kick inside the app
  • Remove Vega / SquawkBot from your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Delete local app data by deleting the .squawkbot folder in your user profile

Children

Vega (SquawkBot) is intended for streamers and is not directed at children under 13.
